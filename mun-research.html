START WITH PRIMARY SOURCES

Research, in a useful order.

Verify the agenda and committee rules with your conference first. Then use these starting points.

01UN Member StatesFind official country and mission starting points.02UN Digital LibrarySearch resolutions, speeches and meeting records.03UN Official DocumentsLocate documents by symbol, date or topic.04UN areas of workUnderstand mandates and issue context.

Source habit: treat a government's statement as evidence of its stated position, not independent proof of every claim. Compare it with UN records, research and reputable reporting.

POSITION PAPER STARTER

Your outline

Use this as a checklist, not a finished position paper. Confirm conference rules, word count and citation format.

  1. Committee mandate and the exact agenda
  2. Country's documented position and national interests
  3. Relevant international law, resolutions and prior action
  4. Evidence-backed proposals within the committee's authority
  5. Sources for each factual claim

BEFORE YOU SPEAK

Delegate source check

  • Can I point to an original document for the country's position?
  • Have I checked what this committee is allowed to do?
  • Did I separate verified facts from my interpretation?
  • Can I name the date and source behind my key claim?
